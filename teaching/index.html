A place to begin

Suggested reading paths

Browse the full library ↓

A few routes through the collection. These are suggestions for independent reading, rather than official course prerequisites; follow the background guidance on each set of notes.

Analysis to variational problems

Build the functional-analytic background, then study minimisers and more general critical points.

  1. Institutions of Analysis

    Start with Hilbert and Banach spaces, weak topologies and Sobolev spaces.

  2. Calculus of Variations

    Use that background for the direct method, relaxation and Γ-convergence.

  3. Variational methods

    Continue to Palais–Smale sequences, mountain-pass and other min-max methods.

Geometry through analysis

An analytic warm-up followed by two complementary viewpoints: rectifiable sets and Lie-group geometry.

  1. Advanced Analysis

    Review distributions and Fourier analysis as an analytic starting point.

  2. Geometric Measure Theory

    Explore Hausdorff measure, densities and rectifiable sets.

  3. Analysis of sublaplacians on Lie groups

    Move to Lie groups, nonisotropic geometry and sublaplacians.

Computation and optimisation

Connect matrix algorithms with the theory and practice of optimisation. The two courses can also be read alongside each other.

  1. Numerical Analysis and Applications

    Study matrix factorizations, eigenvalue algorithms, conditioning and numerical optimisation.

  2. Optimization Theory and Methods

    Take a closer look at convexity, optimality conditions and descent algorithms.
