A little about my work

From analysis
to computation.

My research interests lie in the calculus of variations, geometric measure theory, and applied and numerical analysis. I study shape optimization and free boundaries, analysis on stratified Lie groups, and the spectral growth of matrix families.

I completed my PhD at the Scuola Normale Superiore in Pisa under the supervision of Giuseppe Buttazzo. Since September 2023, I have been at GSSI, working on analytic and computational questions and teaching numerical analysis in the doctoral programme.

Alongside my research, I collect and share lecture notes and build tools such as ArxivLens to make following the literature easier.

Working together

Teaching &
collaboration

At GSSI, I co-lecture in Numerical Analysis and contribute to doctoral research supervision with Nicola Guglielmi. My computational work uses MATLAB, Python, and Fortran.

My postdoctoral research has been supported by collaborative MUR PRIN 2022 and PRIN-PNRR projects, including FIN4GEO. I am affiliated with INdAM–GNCS, the National Group for Scientific Computing.
