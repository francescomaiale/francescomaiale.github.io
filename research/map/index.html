01 · Research area

Variational problems

What makes a shape or a state optimal?

I study shape optimisation and free boundaries, as well as critical points of nonlinear functionals. Compactness, relaxation, and variational methods connect the choice of an admissible object with the equations and geometric structure that emerge.

02 · Research area

Geometric analysis

What does a tangent reveal about a rough set?

I work on rectifiability and harmonic analysis in Heisenberg, Carnot, and homogeneous groups. Measures, tangent models, and intrinsic dilations describe geometric structure beyond the Euclidean setting.

03 · Research area

PDEs & transport

How do equations describe a moving or diffusing system?

Elliptic equations connect the geometry of optimal domains with estimates for their solutions. My computational work also studies time-fractional diffusion, alongside interests in nonlinear mobility. The notes offer background in weak equations, regularity, and transport.

04 · Research area

Computation & matrix growth

How can a computation come with a useful error estimate?

I study spectral growth in matrix families and approximation by constrained neural networks. Numerical linear algebra and reliable bounds also support the computational side of my work on differential equations.

Reading the lines

What connects the areas?

Variational problems ↔ Geometric analysis

Geometric structure

Free-boundary problems ask how an optimal domain is shaped; geometric measure theory supplies ways to measure and describe rough boundaries.

Free-boundary paper → Geometric Measure Theory guide →

Variational problems ↔ PDEs & transport

Energies & weak equations

First variations turn energies into equations. Weak formulations and regularity estimates then help explain the behaviour of solutions and optimal domains.

Calculus of Variations guide → Elliptic Equations guide →

PDEs & transport ↔ Computation & matrix growth

Discretisation & error

Turning a PDE into a numerical method raises questions about approximation, adaptive time steps, and the error introduced by the computation.

Fractional-diffusion talk → Numerical Analysis guide →

Variational problems ↔ Computation & matrix growth

Spectral questions

The reinforcement problem optimises a first eigenvalue. The matrix-family work studies growth of products. Both offer a route into questions about spectral quantities and useful bounds.

Optimal reinforcements → Lower spectral radius →
