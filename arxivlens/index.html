02 / Personal by design

A feed shaped
by your questions.

Follow a broad subject or build a feed for one project. Combine topics with authors, exclude phrases you don’t need, and refine the rules as your interests change.

  • Choose from the arXiv subject catalogue.
  • Match any phrase or require every phrase.
  • Follow an author within your topic or include all their work.
  • Share feed rules with another researcher.

03 / Built for research

Useful beyond the feed.

Notes that stay with a paper

Write a thought, a question, or a next step. Your notes remain attached when a new revision arrives.

A focused reading view

Hide the surrounding panels, adjust the text size, and read equations rendered locally on your Mac.

Your preferred appearance

Choose light or dark mode, colors, and layouts to make the reading space comfortable.

Collections and citations

Keep papers together by project and export BibTeX for a selected paper or a group.

Catch up at your pace

Track unread papers and revisions. Continue a large catch-up in manageable batches.

Export a backup of your library, notes, and settings. Review a restore before applying it.

04 / Privacy

Your library stays
on your Mac.

No account is required. Saved papers, collections, and personal notes are stored locally, with no cloud library sync or AI service.

Feed searches send your chosen subjects, keywords, and author names to arXiv. Cached abstracts and notes are available offline; opening a PDF takes you to arXiv in your browser.

06 / Questions

A few practical details.

Which research fields can I follow?

Any subject in the arXiv catalogue, including mathematics, physics, computer science, statistics, and other covered fields. You can also narrow a feed with phrases or author names.

Does ArxivLens download papers as PDFs?

It keeps paper metadata and abstracts in your local library. PDF links open directly from arXiv in your browser; the app does not automatically download PDFs.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

You can read cached abstracts and work with saved papers and notes offline. Checking feeds and opening online papers requires a connection.

Is this an official arXiv application?

No. ArxivLens is an independent app by Francesco Paolo Maiale. Paper metadata and abstracts come from arXiv.

How can I report a problem or suggest a feature?

Use the app’s Help & Feedback window or email francescopaolo.maiale@gmail.com. Include your macOS version and a short description of what happened.
