Conference · 8–10 October 2026

Fractional diffusion in heterogeneous media

A fast solver for time-fractional diffusion in heterogeneous media, using compressed memory and an adaptive Radau IIA integrator. A residual-based bound controls the linear-solve contribution to concentration error, while synthetic transport experiments in two and three dimensions distinguish retention and shielding effects from numerical error.

NIC60 — Conference in honour of Nicola Guglielmi
Gran Sasso Science Institute, L’Aquila

Fast fractional diffusion in 2D/3D

CMAM 2026 · Vienna

An efficient solver for time-fractional diffusion in two and three dimensions. Exponential approximations reduce the memory cost, while iterative ADI solves and adaptive time steps handle the spatial and temporal computation.

Existence and regularity of the minima of integral shape functionals

Barcelona Analysis Conference · Barcelona

When does an optimal domain exist, and how smooth is its boundary? The talk combines existence results for PDE-constrained integral costs with a blow-up approach to regularity and singular sets.

May 2024

Lower spectral radius · Matrix products · Adaptive algorithms · MATLAB

An algorithm for computing the lower spectral radius

50 Anni di Calcolo delle Variazioni · Pisa

Approximating the slowest asymptotic growth of products from a nonnegative matrix family. The talk develops a Gripenberg-style search with adaptive antinorms and connects the computation to stable switching trajectories.
